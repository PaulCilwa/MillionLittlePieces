1961

October

 We moved to Florida, staying at a rented beach house on St. Augustine Beach while Mom, Gramma and Grandpa went house hunting.

November

 We moved into the house at 312 St. George Street, St. Augustine, Florida.

1971

Jan-Dec

 Attended St. Johns River Junior College (now St. Johns River Community College). Majored in music; teacher was George Champion, and best friends were Mike Tucker, Johanna DeWitt, and Kim Bartlett, with whom I sang in a folk quartet. I dropped out in April of 1972 when I could no longer afford to go.

1972

February

 On February 12, 1972, I married the former Mary Ann Steinberg at the Cathedral of St. Augustine in St. Augustine, Florida. We immediately moved into a garage apartment at 311 Laurel Street, Palatka, FL. I got a part-time job at the landlord's company, Guarantee Title, in addition to the job I had with Mary at the Hungry Pelican fast food restaurant.

May

 Mary and I left the garage apartment and moved in with my best friend, John Griffith, into a travel trailer where, we discovered, the former tenant had murdered his wife with a hatchet. The next day we moved again, into a mobile home at the Breezy Brae trailer park, outside of St. Augustine. While there, I got a job as a tour guide for Palm Coast, which was then a housing development. It's now a city!

June

 Mary and I moved to an apartment on San Sebastian Court in St. Augustine, and I fulfilled a life-long dream when I became a disc jockey for WAOC radio. Unfortunately, the job paid virtually nothing; in December I quit, reluctantly, and we decided to move to Fort Lauderdale.

1973

January

 Mary and I moved to Ft. Lauderdale, staying temporarily with our friends Chris and Terry Palmes. We then got an apartment in a different building, but managed by the same guy, Donald Carney. That was the building in which the hippie couple lived, across the courtyard from us. They had two little boys. The older was six, and his name, which he himself had picked out, was Bobo. His little brother, still a baby, was called "Little Brother" because he was not yet old enough to pick out his own name.

February

 We were unable to pay a second month's rent without a job, and Carney evicted us�even though Mary was very pregnant, and about to give birth. Chris and Terry allowed us to stay in their apartment for a week while they were gone, on condition that Carney not find out. (He did, and Chris and Terry were also evicted.) I worked three days on a construction site, which got enough money to put Mary and me into a room I rented by the week in a woman's home. Although she saw Mary, when Mary had the baby the woman was startled. "I can't have a baby here!" she said, and I was house-hunting again, needing to find a place before Mary got out of the hospital. We rented a small set of rooms in a dilapidated wooden building that would today be called a crack house, again by the week. When we were awakened by a broken beer bottle fight in the hall just outside our door, we left and spent the next several nights with our friends Dick Drolshagen and Brenda Buckenmeyer, on their 24-foot cabin cruiser. The next week we got another by-the-week apartment in the south part of town, infested with cockroaches. A neighbor asked Mary to take her to the bus stop, which she did. When she returned, she found the infant Dorothy crying�Mary had forgotten she had a baby!

1977

Jan-Aug

 I attended St. Johns River Junior College (now St. Johns River Community College) for the "2nd" time. Concurrently, I had a CETA position as a Fire Control Dispatcher for the Florida State Division of Forestry, and had two part time jobs: one with Dobbs Bookbinding, one driving a tour bus for the Old Jail.

Sep

 Moved to Panama City, Florida. Was now a full-time employee of the Florida State Division of Forestry.

1978

Jan

 Began attending The University of West Florida Panama City campus.

Oct

 Moved to Pensacola. I now attended UWF full-time, thanks to my Veterans' benefits.
